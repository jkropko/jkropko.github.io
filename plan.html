Keep it safe

  • Never train through sharp pain — dull effort is fine, sharp is a stop sign.
  • Form over weight. Add load only once the movement is clean.
  • No heavy lifting within ~2 hours of bed — it can keep you wired.
  • Two consistent sessions beat three-then-burnout.
